HI DATA AND RELATED PUBLIC REPORT SOURCES
========================================

Purpose and scope
-----------------
This file organizes the source institutions, access URLs, and report titles supplied by the author. For the influenza B manuscript, the reported HI data sources are limited to the Worldwide Influenza Centre at the Francis Crick Institute and the World Health Organization. The remaining entries are retained as a broader public-resource discovery index and are not claimed as manuscript inputs. This file is not a verified list of the exact HI observations or report editions used in DeepCTS-B.

The author's supplied heading for the New Zealand, ECDC, FDA, and WHO sources refers to influenza A/H3N2 and A/H1N1 HI assay data. Their inclusion here does not establish that they contributed influenza B data to this manuscript. A report may cover multiple virus types, but the relevant tables must be checked individually.

Some documents contain HI results; others primarily contain surveillance summaries, vaccine recommendations, or committee materials. Inclusion in this index does not establish that every listed report contains HI titers. WHO Weekly Epidemiological Record issue ranges are not equivalent to a list of influenza HI data reports.

Titles and dates below are transcribed or consistently formatted from the author-supplied list. They have not all been independently verified against the original documents. Month/year dates are not expanded to invented day-level publication dates. FDA committee dates are retained as supplied and are not asserted to be the publication or revision dates of every associated document. Historical archive URLs are preserved and may redirect or require navigation through the provider's current website.

STUDY-SPECIFIC REPORT AND VERSION RECORDS
---------------------------------------
To document the exact HI sources used in the manuscript, complete data/hi/report_manifest.csv for the reports actually used. For each report, identify:
- Exact document title and issuing institution.
- Original PDF/document URL, not only a provider landing page.
- Publication date and available date precision.
- Revision date or edition, if stated; otherwise record "not stated".
- HI table number(s), page(s), and relevant virus lineage/subtype.
- Historical access/download date, if recoverable; otherwise record "unknown".
- Local source filename and SHA-256 digest, when available.

Do not replace missing publication/version dates with virus collection dates, the repository update date, or an assumed January 1. Do not label all resources in this index as data used for influenza B without checking the study's source records.

Index coverage: 126 author-supplied report or range entries across five providers. This is not a count of 126 verified PDFs: historical and WER ranges are retained as ranges.
