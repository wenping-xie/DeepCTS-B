HI DATA AND RELATED PUBLIC REPORT SOURCES
========================================

Purpose and scope
-----------------
This file lists the two public source families reported for the influenza B manuscript: the Worldwide Influenza Centre at the Francis Crick Institute and the World Health Organization. It is a source catalogue, not a verified list of the exact HI observations or report editions used in DeepCTS-B.

Titles and dates below are transcribed or consistently formatted from the supplied source list. They have not all been independently verified against the original documents. Month/year dates are not expanded to invented day-level publication dates. Historical archive URLs are preserved and may redirect or require navigation through the provider's current website.

STUDY-SPECIFIC REPORT AND VERSION RECORDS
---------------------------------------
To document the exact HI sources used in the manuscript, complete data/hi/report_manifest.csv for the reports actually used. For each report, identify:
- Exact document title and issuing institution.
- Original PDF/document URL, not only a provider landing page.
- Publication date and available date precision.
- Revision date or edition, if stated; otherwise record "not stated".
- HI table number(s), page(s), and relevant virus lineage/subtype.
- Historical access/download date, if recoverable; otherwise record "unknown".
- Local source filename and SHA-256 digest, when available.

Do not replace missing publication/version dates with virus collection dates, the repository update date, or an assumed January 1. Use this manifest only for the WIC and WHO reports actually used for the influenza B manuscript.

The WIC and WHO entries above are source ranges; they are not a count of verified PDFs. Complete the manifest with one row for each report edition actually used.
